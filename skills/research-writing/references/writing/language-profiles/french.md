# [EXTENSION] Language profile — French (fr-FR, with fr-CA / fr-BE / fr-CH notes)

Reference norms: *Le Bon Usage* (Grevisse), Académie française recommendations, *Lexique des règles typographiques en usage à l'Imprimerie nationale* (fr-FR), OQLF *Banque de dépannage linguistique* (fr-CA). Examples are in French because they illustrate French usage.

## 1. Past participle agreement
- With *être*: agrees with the subject (*les données ont été collectées*).
- With *avoir*: agrees with the direct object only when it precedes the verb (*les résultats que nous avons obtenus*; but *nous avons obtenu des résultats*).
- Pronominal verbs: agree with the reflexive pronoun when it is the direct object (*elles se sont inscrites*); no agreement when the pronoun is indirect (*elles se sont posé la question*).
- *fait* + infinitive is invariable; *laissé* + infinitive invariable (1990 rectifications).
- *nous* of modesty (single author): attributes agree with the real author (*nous sommes convaincu*).

## 2. Sequence of tenses and moods
- Indicative after *après que* (*après que les participants ont répondu*); subjunctive after *bien que, quoique, afin que, pour que, avant que, sans que, à moins que, il faut que, il est possible que, il semble que* (but *il me semble que* + indicative).
- *Si* hypothetical: *si* + imperfect → conditional (*si l'échantillon était plus grand, les résultats seraient…*); never *si* + conditional.
- In modern academic prose, the present subjunctive replaces the imperfect subjunctive even after a past main clause (*il fallait que les participants répondent*).
- Tense conventions: *passé composé* (or present) for what was done (*nous avons mesuré*); present for general truths, the paper's contribution and interpretation. Avoid mixing *passé simple* with *passé composé* in the same section.
- Written negation is complete (*ne … pas*); *ne* explétif after *avant que, à moins que, craindre que* is optional but conventional in formal writing.

## 3. Idiomatic constructions and register
- Anglicisms/calques to fix: *supporter* (= support) → *soutenir, prendre en charge*; *impacter* → *avoir une incidence sur, influer sur*; *adresser un problème* → *traiter, aborder*; *définitivement* (= definitely) → *assurément*; *réaliser* (= realize) → *se rendre compte*; *en charge de* → *chargé de*; *faire du sens* → *avoir du sens*; *opportunité* (= chance) → *occasion*; *consistant* (= consistent) → *cohérent*; *digital* → *numérique*; *suite à* → *à la suite de*; *malgré que* → *bien que*.
- Formal register: *en revanche* rather than *par contre*; *toutefois, néanmoins, cependant*; *on* is acceptable but *nous* or impersonal *il* constructions are more formal — keep the author's choice.
- *Il convient de noter que*, *dans ce contexte* repeated: reduce frequency (`../academic-humanization.md`).
- Inclusive writing (*écriture inclusive*, median point): follow the author/venue; never add or remove it unilaterally — flag if inconsistent.

## 4. Typography
| Rule | fr-FR (Imprimerie nationale) | fr-CA (OQLF) |
|---|---|---|
| Space before `:` | non-breaking space | non-breaking space |
| Space before `; ! ?` | narrow non-breaking space | none (or thin space) |
| Guillemets | « texte » with non-breaking spaces inside | « texte » with non-breaking spaces inside |
| Nested quotes | “ ” inside « » | “ ” inside « » |
fr-BE and fr-CH follow mostly fr-FR rules; Swiss usage often omits the space before `; ! ?`. Follow the venue.

- Accented capitals are required (*État, À partir de, ÉTUDE*).
- Decimal comma (*0,05*); thousands separated by a non-breaking space (*10 000*); *%* preceded by a non-breaking space (*15 %*); units with a space (*5 ms*).
- Ordinals: *1er/1re, 2e* (not *2ème*, *2nd* only for a series of two); centuries in small capitals Roman numerals when typography allows (*XXIe siècle*).
- Abbreviations: *p. ex.*, *c.-à-d.*, *cf.*, *etc.* (never followed by "…"); *et al.* in italics per many styles.
- Headings and titles of sections: capital only on the first word and proper nouns.
- 1990 spelling rectifications (*évènement*, *ognon*, no circumflex on *i/u* in many words) are optional: keep the author's system consistently.

## 5. Terminology
- One French term per concept; Commission d'enrichissement / OQLF terms when the venue prefers them (*apprentissage automatique*, *mégadonnées*, *courriel*), otherwise the author's established term.
- English technical terms kept in italics when used, consistently.
