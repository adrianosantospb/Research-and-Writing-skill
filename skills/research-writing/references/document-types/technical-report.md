# Document type: Technical research report

Apply the article structure (`article.md`) — the guideline targets "scientific articles and technical research reports".

## Differences from an article
- Add an **Executive summary** before the Abstract when the audience includes managers; it must state the business problem, the recommendation and the key result in plain language.
- More implementation detail and appendices are acceptable (configurations, full tables, raw-data location).
- Include document control when the organization requires it (owner, reviewers, version, date, classification).
- Business problem (R06) and "was the business problem addressed?" (R31) deserve extra emphasis.
- Brazilian technical/scientific reports may need ABNT NBR 10719 (technical and/or scientific reports) — confirm with the organization.
- R10 (no roadmap paragraph) still applies.
