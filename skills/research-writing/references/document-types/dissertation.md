# [EXTENSION] Document type: Master's dissertation

Pre- and post-textual elements as in `tcc.md` (ABNT NBR 14724 for Brazilian programs, or the university's template).

## Chapter structure
1. **Introduction** — guideline elements R03–R09 + (see `../extensions/objectives-and-research-questions.md`):
   context, problem (business + technical), motivation/justification, research questions and/or hypotheses, general objective, specific objectives, overview of the proposal and of the evaluation (with positive and negative results), contributions, (resulting publications).
   **"Organization of the work" paragraph: ask the writer** — the guideline forbids it for articles, but many programs expect it in dissertations. Record the decision and apply it consistently.
2. **Background (theoretical foundation)** — `../extensions/theoretical-foundation.md`.
3. **Related work** — R11–R13 (may also be a systematic mapping/review: `../extensions/systematic-review.md`).
4. **Proposed solution** — §3.1 may become its own chapter when large.
5. **Method and evaluation design** — §3.2–3.4 (R15–R19).
6. **Results** — R20–R22.
7. **Discussion** — R23–R28.
8. **Conclusion** — R29–R32, with an explicit table or paragraph mapping each specific objective / RQ → where it was answered → status.

## Traceability matrix (recommended)
| Specific objective / RQ / H | Method section | Result section | Discussion section | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Notes
- Each chapter may open with a short paragraph stating its purpose (allowed; it is not the document-level roadmap).
- Papers published during the program can be listed and mapped to chapters.
- Write chapter headings in the manuscript language.
