# Vancouver (ICMJE / NLM)

Biomedical and health journals (relevant to the guideline's Health Technology area). Many journals apply small variations — always check the journal's "Instructions for authors".

## In-text
- Numeric, in order of first citation: superscript¹ or in brackets/parentheses [1] (1), per journal. Ranges: 1–3; lists: 1,4,6.

## Reference list — format examples (fictitious)
- Journal: Silva A, Smith J, Wong L. Title of the article. J Abbrev Name. 2024;12(3):45-60. doi:10.xxxx/xxxxx
- Book: Silva A. Title of the book. 2nd ed. City: Publisher; 2023.
- Chapter: Silva A. Title of chapter. In: Smith J, editor. Title of book. City: Publisher; 2023. p. 10-25.
- Webpage: Organization. Title of page [Internet]. City: Publisher; 2024 [cited 2026 Nov 10]. Available from: URL
- Up to 6 authors, then "et al."; journal titles abbreviated per NLM catalog; no italics.

## Tools
LaTeX: `vancouver.bst` (natbib numeric) or `\usepackage[style=vancouver]{biblatex}` where available. Zotero/Mendeley: CSL "Vancouver" or the journal's own CSL.
