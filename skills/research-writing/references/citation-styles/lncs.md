# Springer LNCS (Lecture Notes in Computer Science)

Many computing conferences publishing with Springer (LNCS, LNAI, LNBI, CCIS).

## In-text
- Numeric in square brackets, ordered alphabetically or by citation according to the template (`splncs04` sorts alphabetically by default): [1], [2,5].

## Reference list — format examples (fictitious)
- Journal: Silva, A., Smith, J.: Title of the article. Journal Name **12**(3), 45–60 (2024). https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx
- Proceedings: Silva, A., Smith, J.: Title of the paper. In: Wong, L. (ed.) CONF 2024. LNCS, vol. 14000, pp. 1–15. Springer, Cham (2024). https://doi.org/10.1007/xxxxx
- Book: Silva, A.: Title of the Book, 2nd edn. Publisher, City (2023)
- Online: Title of page, URL. Last accessed 10 Nov 2026
- Surname, initials; volume in bold; year in parentheses at the end.

## Tools
LaTeX: `\documentclass{llncs}` + `\bibliographystyle{splncs04}`. Zotero/Mendeley: CSL "Springer - Lecture Notes in Computer Science".
