# [EXTENSION] Objectives, research questions and hypotheses

Not in the original guideline (which uses hypotheses only); required by most theses, dissertations, TCCs and proposals.

## General objective
One sentence, infinitive verb, the whole scope: "To propose and evaluate [solution] for [technical problem] in [context]."

## Specific objectives
3–6 items, each verifiable and mapped to a method step and a result. Use action verbs matching the cognitive level:
identify, characterize, survey (exploratory) · describe, compare, classify (descriptive) · implement, develop, propose (technical) · evaluate, measure, test, analyze (empirical).
Avoid non-verifiable verbs: "understand", "know", "study", "contribute to".

## Research questions (RQs)
- Main RQ derived from the technical problem; sub-RQs (RQ1, RQ2…) derived from specific objectives.
- Each RQ should be answerable by the evaluation design; quantitative RQs map to H0/H1 pairs.
- Example: RQ1 — "Does method A achieve higher F1-score than baseline B on dataset D?" → H0₁/H1₁.

## Consistency checks
| Item | Must connect to |
|---|---|
| General objective | Technical problem (R06) |
| Each specific objective | A Method step and a Results section |
| Each RQ | A hypothesis (if quantitative) or an analysis procedure (if qualitative) |
| Each hypothesis | Evaluated in the Discussion (R24) |
| All of them | Answered in the Conclusions (R31) |
