# [EXTENSION] Theoretical foundation (Background)

Common in TCCs, dissertations, theses and proposals; usually absent or brief in articles.

## Purpose
Give the reader the concepts, theories, techniques and standards needed to understand the proposal and the evaluation. It is **not** Related Work: background explains *concepts*; Related Work analyzes *competing studies* (R11–R13).

## How to build it
1. List every concept used in the Method and Results that a reader of the target committee might not know.
2. Order from general to specific, ending with the concepts closest to the proposal.
3. For each concept: definition (with citation to primary or canonical sources), key properties/variants, and **why it matters for this work** (one sentence linking back).
4. Close the chapter with a short synthesis connecting the concepts to the problem.

## Common mistakes
- Textbook-style chapters disconnected from the proposal.
- Citing only secondary sources/websites for core definitions.
- Including concepts never used later.
