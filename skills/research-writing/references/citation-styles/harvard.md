# Harvard (author–date family)

"Harvard" is not a single standard: universities and publishers use variants (e.g., *Cite Them Right*, Elsevier Harvard). **Ask which variant**; default to Cite Them Right.

## In-text
- (Silva, 2024) · (Silva, 2024, p. 45) · (Silva and Smith, 2024) · four or more authors: (Silva et al., 2024) — the threshold varies by variant.

## Reference list — format examples (fictitious, Cite Them Right style)
- Journal: Silva, A., Smith, J. and Wong, L. (2024) 'Title of the article', *Journal Name*, 12(3), pp. 45–60. Available at: https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx.
- Book: Silva, A. (2023) *Title of the book*. 2nd edn. City: Publisher.
- Chapter: Silva, A. (2023) 'Title of chapter', in Smith, J. (ed.) *Title of book*. City: Publisher, pp. 10–25.
- Webpage: Organization (2024) *Title of page*. Available at: URL (Accessed: 10 November 2026).
- Alphabetical order by author surname.

## Tools
LaTeX: `\usepackage[style=authoryear]{biblatex}` adjusted to the variant, or natbib with `agsm`/`dcu`. Zotero/Mendeley: CSL "Cite Them Right – Harvard" or "Elsevier - Harvard (with titles)".
