# APA Style (7th edition)

Social sciences, education, psychology, many health and information-systems journals.

## In-text (author–date)
- Parenthetical: (Silva, 2024) · (Silva & Smith, 2024) · three or more authors: (Silva et al., 2024).
- Narrative: Silva and Smith (2024) found…
- Direct quotes need a page: (Silva, 2024, p. 12); quotes of 40+ words as a block quotation.
- Multiple works: (Silva, 2023; Smith, 2024) alphabetical.

## Reference list — format examples (fictitious)
- Journal: Silva, A., Smith, J., & Wong, L. (2024). Title of the article in sentence case. *Journal Name in Title Case, 12*(3), 45–60. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx
- Book: Silva, A. (2023). *Title of the book in sentence case* (2nd ed.). Publisher.
- Chapter: Silva, A. (2023). Title of chapter. In J. Smith (Ed.), *Title of book* (pp. 10–25). Publisher.
- Conference paper (published in proceedings): treat as journal article or chapter depending on publication form.
- Webpage: Organization. (2024, May 3). *Title of page*. Site Name. URL
- Up to 20 authors listed; DOI as URL; hanging indent; alphabetical order.

## Tools
LaTeX: `\usepackage[style=apa]{biblatex}` (biblatex-apa). Zotero/Mendeley: CSL "American Psychological Association 7th edition".
