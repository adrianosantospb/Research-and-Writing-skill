# [EXTENSION] Document type: Research proposal / qualification exam

A proposal describes **planned** work, so it reuses the guideline up to the Method and replaces Results/Discussion with plans.

## Structure
1. Introduction — R03–R08 + objectives, research questions/hypotheses, expected contributions. R09 becomes "planned evaluation" (and preliminary results, if any).
2. Theoretical foundation (`../extensions/theoretical-foundation.md`).
3. Related work — R11–R13 (table last row = the proposal as planned).
4. Proposed solution — as designed so far.
5. Methodology and evaluation plan — R15–R18, written in the future tense; include the planned statistical analysis.
6. **Preliminary results** (qualification) — clearly labeled as preliminary; same no-interpretation rule in the results part.
7. **Work plan and schedule** — activities × months (Gantt-style table); risks and mitigation.
8. Expected results and contributions.
9. References.

## Notes
- Funding proposals (CNPq, FAPESP, CAPES, etc.) have their own mandatory sections and limits — ask for the call (edital) and follow it.
- Ethics approval must be planned before data collection with humans (`../extensions/ethics-data-and-ai-disclosure.md`).
