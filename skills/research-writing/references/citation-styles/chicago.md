# Chicago Manual of Style — author-date (18th edition, 2024)

Humanities and social sciences; some interdisciplinary journals. (Chicago also has a notes-bibliography system — ask which one.)

## In-text (author-date)
- (Silva 2024) · (Silva 2024, 45) · (Silva and Smith 2024) · three or more authors: (Silva et al. 2024). No comma between author and year.

## Reference list — format examples (fictitious)
- Journal: Silva, Ana, John Smith, and Lee Wong. 2024. "Title of the Article." *Journal Name* 12 (3): 45–60. https://doi.org/10.xxxx/xxxxx.
- Book: Silva, Ana. 2023. *Title of the Book*. 2nd ed. City: Publisher.
- Chapter: Silva, Ana. 2023. "Title of Chapter." In *Title of Book*, edited by John Smith, 10–25. City: Publisher.
- Webpage: Organization. 2024. "Title of Page." Site Name. May 3, 2024. URL.
- Headline-style capitalization; full first names; alphabetical order.

## Tools
LaTeX: `biblatex-chicago` (`\usepackage[authordate]{biblatex-chicago}`). Zotero/Mendeley: CSL "Chicago Manual of Style 18th edition (author-date)" (or 17th if the journal still requires it).
