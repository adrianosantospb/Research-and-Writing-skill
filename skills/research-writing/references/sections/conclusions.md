# Conclusions

Rules: R29–R32, C5. Purpose: summarize the overall contribution at a higher level than the Discussion.

## Required elements
1. **Research summary** — the problem investigated, the proposed solution, the evaluation performed (short).
2. **Main conclusions** — final conclusions derived from the evidence.
3. **Contributions** — state explicitly and separately:
   - Scientific contributions (new knowledge, method, model, evidence);
   - Technical contributions (artifact, tool, algorithm, dataset, implementation);
   - Practical contributions (impact for organizations/users/society).
   Explain why the work is significant.
4. **Objectives check** — answer explicitly:
   - Were the original objectives achieved? (map each specific objective when they exist)
   - Was the research (technical) problem solved?
   - Was the business problem addressed?
5. **Limitations and future work** — remaining limitations, open challenges, possible improvements, future directions, including **new research questions, new hypotheses and future experiments**.

## Common mistakes
- Introducing results or citations not discussed before.
- Generic future work ("expand the dataset") without a concrete question or hypothesis.
- Not answering whether the business problem was addressed.
