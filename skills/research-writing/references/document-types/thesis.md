# [EXTENSION] Document type: Doctoral thesis

Use the dissertation structure (`dissertation.md`) with these additional demands:

1. **Thesis statement** — one or two sentences stating the central claim the thesis defends ("This thesis argues that…"). Place it in the Introduction and revisit it in the Conclusion.
2. **Originality** — the contributions must be original and defensible; the Related Work must show the gap rigorously (a systematic review/mapping is common — `../extensions/systematic-review.md`).
3. **Multiple studies** — theses often have several studies/experiments. For each study keep the Method → Results → Discussion separation, or organize per study with a final integrative discussion.
4. **Contributions chapter or section** — scientific, technical and practical contributions (R30), plus publications and artifacts (datasets, tools) produced.
5. **Threats to validity** at the thesis level, not only per experiment.
6. **Article-based thesis (compilation format)** — when the program allows: introduction and integrative chapters + papers as chapters. Introduction and conclusion must still satisfy R03–R09 and R29–R32 for the whole thesis; check copyright permissions for published papers.
7. **"Organization of the work" paragraph** — writer's decision (ask).
