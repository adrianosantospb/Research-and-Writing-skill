# IEEE Reference Style

Used by IEEE conferences and transactions (engineering, computing).

## In-text
- Numeric in square brackets, numbered in order of first citation: [1], [2]–[4], [1], [5].
- The number works as a noun or tag: "as shown in [3]" / "Silva *et al.* [3] propose…".

## Reference list — format examples (fictitious)
- Journal: [1] A. Silva, J. Smith, and L. Wong, "Title of the article," *IEEE Trans. Softw. Eng.*, vol. 50, no. 4, pp. 100–115, Apr. 2024, doi: 10.1109/TSE.2024.xxxxxxx.
- Conference: [2] A. Silva and J. Smith, "Title of the paper," in *Proc. IEEE Int. Conf. Data Mining (ICDM)*, City, Country, 2024, pp. 1–10, doi: ….
- Book: [3] A. Silva, *Title of the Book*, 2nd ed. City, Country: Publisher, 2023.
- Online: [4] A. Silva, "Title of page," Site Name. Accessed: Nov. 10, 2026. [Online]. Available: URL
- Initials before surname; up to 6 authors listed (more: first author + *et al.*); abbreviated journal names per IEEE list; ordered by citation number.

## Tools
LaTeX: `IEEEtran` class + `\bibliographystyle{IEEEtran}`. Zotero/Mendeley: CSL "IEEE".
