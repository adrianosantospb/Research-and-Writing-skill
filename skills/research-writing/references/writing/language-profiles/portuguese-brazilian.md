# [EXTENSION] Language profile — Brazilian Portuguese (pt-BR)

Reference norms: normative grammar (e.g., Bechara, Cunha & Cintra), *VOLP* (Academia Brasileira de Letras) for spelling under the 1990 Orthographic Agreement, and ABNT for academic presentation when applicable (`../../citation-styles/abnt.md`, `../../document-types/tcc.md`).
Examples below are in Portuguese because they illustrate Portuguese usage.

## 1. Agreement (concordância)
- **Verbal**
  - *haver* meaning "exist" and *fazer* indicating elapsed time are impersonal → singular: *houve problemas*, *faz dois anos* (not *houveram*, *fazem*). *existir* agrees: *existem problemas*.
  - Collective/partitive subjects (*a maioria dos participantes*) accept singular or plural; keep one choice consistently.
  - *mais de um* → singular; *um dos que* → plural preferred (*um dos métodos que apresentaram*).
  - Percentages: the verb agrees with the number or with the specifier (*30% dos participantes responderam*); be consistent.
  - Passive with *se*: *Realizaram-se os testes* / *Foram realizados os testes* (verb agrees with the patient subject). Indeterminate subject with indirect object stays singular: *Trata-se de métodos*.
- **Nominal**: *anexo/incluso/obrigado/mesmo* agree (*seguem anexas as tabelas*); *é necessário paciência* (invariable with undetermined noun) vs *é necessária a paciência*; *menos* and *haja vista* are invariable; *meio* adverb is invariable (*meio confusa*).

## 2. Government (regência)
| Verb/noun | Formal academic use |
|---|---|
| *visar* (aim) | *visa a* + noun/infinitive (direct use is accepted by many grammars; keep consistent) |
| *implicar* (entail) | direct: *implica mudanças* (not *implica em*) |
| *preferir* | *preferir A a B* (not *do que*) |
| *assistir* (watch/attend) | *assistir a* |
| *proceder* (carry out) | *proceder à análise* |
| *chegar*, *ir* | *chegar a* (not *em*) |
| *obedecer, responder, aspirar* | + *a* |
| *ao encontro de* (in agreement) vs *de encontro a* (against) | meaning differs — check intent `[AUTHOR CHECK]` |
| *em vez de* (instead) vs *ao invés de* (opposite) | use *em vez de* for substitution |

**Crase**: *a* (preposition) + *a* (article) before feminine nouns: *devido à falta*, *em relação à proposta*, *às vezes*. No crase before masculine nouns, verbs, *a partir de*, *a uma*, indefinite pronouns; optional before feminine possessives and female first names.

## 3. Pronoun placement (colocação pronominal)
- Formal written norm: do not start a sentence with an unstressed pronoun (*Me parece* → *Parece-me*).
- Attractors require proclisis: negatives (*não se observou*), relative pronouns (*que se obteve*), certain adverbs and conjunctions.
- Proclisis after an explicit subject is standard in Brazilian academic prose (*O método se baseia em…*); do not "correct" it to enclisis.
- Future tenses: mesoclisis (*realizar-se-á*) is correct but rare; prefer rephrasing (*será realizado*) or proclisis with an attractor.

## 4. Punctuation
- Never separate subject from verb or verb from its complement with a comma.
- Commas around explanatory clauses and appositives; none around restrictive clauses (meaning differs → `[AUTHOR CHECK]` if unclear).
- Comma after long or displaced adverbial adjuncts (*Neste trabalho, …* optional for short ones; keep the author's habit consistent).
- Comma before *mas*, *porém* in mid-sentence; *porém/contudo/entretanto* between commas when displaced.
- *e/ou*: acceptable in technical writing; do not overuse.

## 5. Spelling and orthography
- Post-1990 forms: *autoestima*, *infraestrutura*, *coorientador*, *microssistema*, *anti-inflamatório*, *semiautomático*, *pós-graduação*, *micro-ondas*. Check doubtful compounds in VOLP.
- *por que / porque / por quê / porquê*.
- *a fim de* (purpose) vs *afim* (related); *senão* vs *se não*; *mal* vs *mau*; *há* (time elapsed) vs *a* (future/distance).
- Keep established English technical terms in italics when the author/venue does so (*dataset*, *deep learning*), consistently.

## 6. Academic conventions
- **Voice**: impersonal (*realizou-se*, *foram realizados*) or first person plural (*realizamos*) — keep the author's choice; do not mix.
- **Tenses**: Method and Results in *pretérito perfeito* (*foram coletados*); established knowledge in present; the paper itself in present (*Este trabalho propõe*).
- **Demonstratives**: *este* for the current text/what follows (*neste trabalho*), *esse* for what was just mentioned.
- **Avoid**: *onde* for non-places (→ *em que*, *no qual*); *o mesmo/a mesma* as anaphoric pronoun (→ *ele*, *este*, or repeat the noun); gerundism (*vamos estar analisando*); *a nível de* (→ *em nível de*, *no âmbito de*); *sendo que* as a generic connective; *realizar* meaning "perceive" (anglicism → *perceber*).
- **Numbers**: decimal comma (*0,05*), thousands separator point or space (follow venue; be consistent); units with a space (*10 kg*, *5 ms*); percentage format per venue (*15%* is common in Brazil), consistent.
- **ABNT presentation** (when applicable): *Tabela 1*, *Figura 2*, *Quadro 3* capitalized; citations per NBR 10520:2023 (mixed-case author names in parentheses); direct quotations over three lines as an indented block.

## 7. Common transfer errors to watch (from English sources)
*performance* (accepted, but *desempenho* preferred in formal text), *assumir* meaning "suppose" (→ *supor*, *pressupor*), *endereçar um problema* (→ *tratar*, *abordar*), *suportar* meaning "support" (→ *dar suporte a*, *apoiar*, *ser compatível com*), *eventualmente* meaning "eventually" (→ *por fim*), *atualmente* is correct for "currently".
