# [EXTENSION] Survey / systematic review research

Use when the research nature is "survey or review" (methodology-taxonomy) or when Related Work is a systematic mapping/review.

## Reference guidelines (choose by area)
- **PRISMA 2020** (health and general systematic reviews; flow diagram + checklist).
- **Kitchenham & Charters** guidelines for systematic literature reviews in software engineering; **Petersen et al.** for systematic mapping studies.
- Snowballing (Wohlin) as complement or alternative to database search.
- Ask which one the venue/program expects; cite it in the Method.

## Mapping onto the guideline structure
| Guideline section | Review equivalent |
|---|---|
| Introduction | Motivation for the review; need (no previous review or outdated); RQs |
| Related Work | Previous reviews/secondary studies and their gaps |
| 3.1 Proposed solution | Review protocol (instead of an artifact) |
| 3.2 Methodology | Classified as survey/review research, bibliographic object (prose) |
| 3.4 Planning | Search string, databases, period, inclusion/exclusion criteria, selection stages, quality assessment, data extraction form, synthesis method, protocol registration (e.g., PROSPERO for health) |
| Results | Selection numbers (flow diagram), study characteristics, answers per RQ — no interpretation |
| Discussion | Interpretation per RQ, implications, gaps for research |
| Threats | Search coverage, selection bias, extraction reliability (inter-rater agreement), publication bias |

Never invent counts of retrieved/included studies (I2).
