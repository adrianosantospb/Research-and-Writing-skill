# Introduction

Rules: R03–R10, C1, C5. Purpose: establish context and motivate the study.

## Required building blocks (suggested paragraph order)
1. **General statement of the research area** (R03) — the broader domain and why it matters.
2. **Specific problem** (R04) — the precise problem; its difficulty, existing challenges, limitations of current approaches.
3. **Importance and motivation** (R05, R06)
   - Who is affected? What happens if it stays unresolved? Why are current solutions insufficient?
   - **Business problem**: practical motivation, organizational impact, real-world need.
   - **Technical problem**: the computational/methodological/scientific challenge.
   - State both explicitly and separately — reviewers will look for them.
4. **Existing approaches and limitations** (R07) — brief; the detailed critique belongs to Related Work.
5. **Proposed solution overview** (R08) — high level, no implementation detail.
6. **Evaluation overview** (R09) — how it was evaluated and the main positive **and** negative results.

[EXTENSION] For dissertations, theses and proposals, add (see `extensions/objectives-and-research-questions.md`):
- General objective and specific objectives; research questions and/or hypotheses; expected contributions.

## Roadmap paragraph (R10)
- Articles and reports: **do not** write "Section 2 presents…, Section 3…". Referring to a specific section where useful is fine ("details in Section 3.4").
- Dissertations/theses: **ask the writer** whether their program expects an "Organization of the work" paragraph. Follow the writer's decision and record it.

## Questions to ask the author when information is missing
- Who suffers from this problem in practice, and what does it cost them?
- What, precisely, cannot be done today (technical problem)?
- What are the 2–3 best existing approaches and their main limitation?
- How was the solution evaluated, and what did not work as expected?

## Common mistakes
- Mixing business and technical problems in one vague sentence.
- Hiding negative results.
- A long literature review inside the Introduction.
- Ending with the structure paragraph (articles).

## Self-check
- [ ] Business problem stated? [ ] Technical problem stated?
- [ ] Positive and negative results mentioned?
- [ ] No roadmap paragraph (articles/reports)?
