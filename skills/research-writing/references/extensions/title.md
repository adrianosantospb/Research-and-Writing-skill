# [EXTENSION] Title

- State the contribution or problem + approach + context, in ~10–15 words for articles.
  Patterns: "[Approach] for [problem] in [context]"; "[Approach]: [main finding/claim]"; "Evaluating [X] for [Y]: [study type]".
- Include the 1–2 most important keywords early (search engines and indexers weigh them).
- Avoid unexplained acronyms, hype ("novel", "revolutionary"), and questions unless the venue favors them.
- Theses/dissertations may use title + subtitle (ABNT: separated by a colon).
- Offer 3–5 alternatives with different emphases (problem-focused, solution-focused, result-focused).
