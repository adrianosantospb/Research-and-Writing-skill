# Document type: Scientific article (conference or journal)

This is the guideline's native format — apply `core-rules.md` fully, including R10 (no roadmap paragraph).

## Structure
Title · Abstract · Categories/General terms/Keywords · 1 Introduction · 2 Related Work · 3 Method (3.1 Proposed solution, 3.2 Research methodology, 3.3 Models and frameworks, 3.4 Experimental planning) · 4 Results · 5 Discussion · 6 Conclusions · References · (Appendices / Acknowledgments / Data availability as required by the venue).

## Venue adaptations
- Respect page/word limits: compress Related Work into thematic paragraphs + comparative table; keep the 4-part logic for each study even if each part is one sentence.
- Results + Discussion merged only if the venue requires (R22).
- Some venues place Related Work after Discussion; keep the content rules regardless of position.
- Double-blind review: anonymize self-citations ("[Anonymous, 2025]"), project names, links and acknowledgments.
- Typical template/style pairs: ACM (acmart) → ACM style; IEEE (IEEEtran) → IEEE style; Springer (llncs) → LNCS style; Elsevier/Health → Vancouver or APA per journal; Brazilian venues (SBC, etc.) → follow the event template (often SBC template; ABNT for some journals).

## Short-paper variant (4–6 pages)
Method §3.2–3.4 may be condensed into one "Evaluation design" subsection, but hypotheses, variables and threats must still appear.
